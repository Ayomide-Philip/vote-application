Spacing and radius

Style 3 Editorial Grid & Ledger Architecture: Boxed, structured cells with strict 0px geometry and 1.5px / 2px hairline junctions. Echoes the rigor, permanence, and transparency of official legislative records, parliamentary gazettes, and printed ballot papers.

Buttons

Style 3 Editorial Grid: Boxed ledger cells with 1.5px architectural frames, letterspaced Satoshi bold uppercase typography, and stark high-contrast hover inversions. Hover over any button to preview the ink-stamp inversion.

Fields

Inputs for entering unique election codes and verifying institutional accounts.

Radio selectors for single-choice positions and checkboxes for multi-seat representation.
